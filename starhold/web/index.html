STARHOLD

How to play

Scrap mechs march down the road to your reactor core. Each one that gets there costs lives. Lose them all and the run is over.

Tap a glowing pad to see the towers. Tap one to see what it does, and tap it again to build it. With a mouse, point at it and click. Tap a built tower to see it, upgrade it or sell it. Each upgrade hits harder and reaches further. At level 3, pick one of its two final forms.

Blasters and Tesla coils hit flying Buzzers. Missile pods can't. A Drone bay's drones wall off the road: ground robots pile up there until the drones fall. Tap a robot to see what it is.

Between waves, a robot head on the road counts down to the next one. Tap it to see what's coming, and tap again to call it early for bonus money.

Tap Strike or Stasis in the bottom corners, then tap the map to fire it there.

Each kill scores its points times the wave it came in. Every 10th wave brings a Scrap Colossus.

        Payouts

        After a run, players can cash in its score: each point pays the rate, up to the most per cash-in. Starting another run gives up a score that wasn't cashed in, so players save their cash-ins for their best runs. Cash-ins come back at midnight UTC.

        The bank is global, so these payouts apply in every server, and cash-ins count across servers.
