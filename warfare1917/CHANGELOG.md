# Warfare 1917 Changelog

## 0.2.0

Needs ActivityHub 0.10.0 or later.

- Everyone in a Discord server now plays together, whichever voice channel they opened the Activity in. The Watch list shows every battle in the server, and the Versus list every open game.
- Battles and games of people in your own voice channel are at the top of both lists.
- When you open a versus game, pick who can take it: **Whole server** or **My channel**. Whole server is picked each time. A My channel game only shows in your own channel's list, and says so on your row. Once its battle starts, anyone in the server can watch it.
- The versus setup no longer shows the A.I. setting. A person commands the other side, so it did nothing.
- Moving to another voice channel in the same server no longer loses your versus battle, and your open game stays open while you have the game open anywhere in the server.

## 0.1.3

- The game now writes problems on a player's device to the bot's log: page errors, the graphics being lost or coming back, and the drawing stopping. This helps track down a black screen after the Activity shrinks into its small window.

## 0.1.2

- Fixed the battle freezing for good when a tank was destroyed. Some wrecks hid one of their fires, and the drawing crashed as the wreck faded in.
- Fixed reopening a battle that has a destroyed tank in it. The battle no longer shows only the empty field.

## 0.1.1

- Pressing the Gas Masks button now shows a "GAS MASKS ON" message, and the button reads "MASKS ON" while your squads wear masks.

## 0.1.0

First release. Warfare 1917, the 2008 Flash game by Con Artist Games and Armor Games, with its original art, sounds and music.

- Lead the British or German army across the trenches of 1917. Deploy riflemen, machine guns, officers, tanks and more, call in fire support, take the enemy's trenches and keep your morale up.
- Both campaigns: nine missions as the British and nine as the Germans. Win to move on, lose to retry. Finish the ninth for the newspaper and your campaign stats.
- Earn experience from kills and spend it on the original's upgrade trees. The upgrades that were broken in the original now work as described.
- Custom Battle: choose your side, difficulty, units, supports, map size, trenches, wire and mines.
- Play alone, watch other battles in the same Activity, or fight a friend in a 1 vs 1 battle.
- One campaign save per Discord account, kept across servers and devices.
- Works on desktop and on phones held upright or sideways. The sponsor intros, ads and links are gone.
- No Red currency payouts.
