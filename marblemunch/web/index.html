      Payouts

      After a round, players can cash in their marbles: each marble pays the rate, up to the most per cash-in. Their next round replaces an unsent one, so players save their cash-ins for their best rounds. Cash-ins come back at midnight UTC.

      The bank is global, so these payouts apply in every server, and cash-ins count across servers.
