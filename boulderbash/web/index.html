Boulder Bash

How to play

Tap a clump of two or more touching blocks of the same color to pick it. Tap it again to smash it.

Blocks above fall down, and empty columns slide together. Bigger clumps score more for every block.

A level ends when no clump of two is left. Reach the level's goal to go on to the next one.

Stone blocks never match. Only a Bomb clears them.

Modes

Zen
Ten relaxed levels, with no goals and no score.
Normal
Ten levels. Reach each level's goal to go on.
Extreme
One tap smashes. New rows push up, faster and faster. Don't let a column reach the top.
Normal x2
Two boards at once, each with its own goals. Play on until both are done.
Extreme x2
Two Extreme boards. The game ends when either one fills up.

Special blocks

  • Refill drops a new block into every column with room.
  • Countdown counts down from 15. At 0, its whole clump turns to stone.
  • Triple scores its clump three times over.
  • Bomb: tap it to smash everything around it.
  • Color wipe also smashes every other block of its color.

Special blocks

  • Shifter changes color every 6 seconds.
  • Wild joins a clump of any color. It can't be tapped on its own.
  • Undo charges the Undo slot, which takes back your last move.
  • Shuffle charges the Shuffle slot, which mixes up the blocks.
  • Column hold (Extreme only): its column skips the next rising row.

    Payouts

    After a game, players can cash in its score: each point pays the rate, up to the most per cash-in. Starting another game gives up a score that wasn't cashed in, so players save their cash-ins for their best games. Cash-ins come back at midnight UTC.

    The bank is global, so these payouts apply in every server, and cash-ins count across servers.
