# The Last Stand: Union City Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release. The Last Stand: Union City is a side-view zombie survival RPG from Con Artist Games, brought into ActivityHub with its original art and sounds.

- **The whole story.** Make a survivor (name, look, an occupation or your own skills), pick Run & Gun or Survivor mode, and fight your way across Union City to find your partner and escape by boat. All 11 areas, the quests, shops, companions, weapons, 12 skills and the ending are in.
- **Three save slots that follow you.** Your stories are kept by the bot, so they are the same on every server and on every device, and a cleared browser cache does not lose them. If another device saved the same story more recently, the game says SAVED ELSEWHERE and Continue picks up the newer save. If a save can't reach the bot, SAVE FAILED shows and you can try again; pressing Back twice leaves without saving.
- **Desktop and phones.** Mouse and keyboard on a computer. On a phone held sideways, a move stick, an aim stick (push to the edge to fire) and buttons for the rest.
- **The original's bugs are fixed.** Crashes, stuck screens and wrong text from the Flash version are gone, and the game plays with the original's numbers.
- **No sponsor bits.** The logo intro, Armor Games login, ads and social prompts are left out, and everything that was premium is open to everyone.
- **See who is here.** The hub's people button sits in the game's top bar, and friends' faces show next to their area on the city map. Everyone plays their own story, side by side.
- **No currency.** The game pays no server currency.
