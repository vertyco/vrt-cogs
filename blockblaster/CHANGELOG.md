# Block Blaster Changelog

- First release: Block Blaster for ActivityHub, a remake of the phone puzzle Pixel Flow! in live 3D with all 2,600 levels, its boosters, progress kept by the bot on every server, watching a friend live, and an optional credit shop for boosters that admins turn on. Levels 1 to 599 have every twist; the twists from level 600 on are still being built.
