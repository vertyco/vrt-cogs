# Warfare 1944 Changelog

## 0.2.0

Needs ActivityHub 0.10.0 or later.

- Everyone in a Discord server now plays together, whichever voice channel they opened the Activity in. The Watch list shows every battle in the server, and the Versus list every open game.
- Battles and games of people in your own voice channel are at the top of both lists.
- When you open a versus game, pick who can take it: **Whole server** or **My channel**. Whole server is picked each time. A My channel game only shows in your own channel's list, and says so on your row. Once its battle starts, anyone in the server can watch it.
- Moving to another voice channel in the same server no longer loses your versus battle, and your open game stays open while you have the game open anywhere in the server.

## 0.1.1

- The Versus and Watch screens' buttons now work: the list of games no longer covers them.

## 0.1.0

First release. Warfare 1944, the 2009 Flash game by Con Artist Games and Armor Games, with its original art, sounds and music.

- Lead the American or German army across the battlefields of 1944. Spend resource points to deploy riflemen, assault teams, machine guns, snipers, bazookas, mortars, officers and tanks in three lanes, and keep your morale up.
- Select a squad to give it orders: advance, throw a grenade or smoke, or call in artillery, air support and smoke rounds.
- Squads take cover behind walls, fences, sandbags and log piles. Win by morale, by pushing through, by flanking, or by taking the mission's objective.
- Both campaigns: eight missions as the Americans and eight as the Germans. Win to move on, lose to retry. Finish the last one for your campaign stats.
- Earn experience from kills and spend it on the original's upgrade trees. The upgrades that were broken in the original now work as described.
- Custom Battle: choose your side, difficulty, units, supports, map and starting resource points.
- The original's narrated tutorial.
- Play alone, watch other battles in the same Activity, or fight a friend in a 1 vs 1 battle.
- One campaign save per Discord account, kept across servers and devices.
- Works on desktop and on phones held upright or sideways. The sponsor intros, ads and links are gone.
- No Red currency payouts.
