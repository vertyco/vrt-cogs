# FarmCord Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release: a small FarmVille-style farming game for ActivityHub, with game design by Vainne. Each player has one farm that follows them to every server and DM, with a farmhouse, a Yard, a Pond, a Market and seven plots to walk between. Players grow 16 crops through week-long seasons and rainy days, raise, breed and sell six kinds of animals, climb 100 levels that unlock plots, tools, storage and two extra farmhouse rooms, earn 15 achievement badges, decorate the farm, dress the farmer and compete on a global leaderboard. The bot keeps the clock and rolls every random result, so crops grow and animals get hungry while players are away. Server admins can let players turn FarmCoins into the server's credits at the Market's bank counter, Neopets style (off until turned on). The art is Zynga's FarmVille art, with a few pieces made with Codex image generation, and farms are kept in SQLite.
