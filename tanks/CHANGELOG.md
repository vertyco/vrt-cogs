# Tanks Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release: turn-based artillery for ActivityHub, ported from 2DPlay's Flash game Tanks. Up to five players and computer tanks, all 14 weapons and 12 shop items, three landscapes, a time limit for turns and the shop that the host sets (1 minute by default, or no limit; none for one person against computers), and a global leaderboard ranked by match wins.
